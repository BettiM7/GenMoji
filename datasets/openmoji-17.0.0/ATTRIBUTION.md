# OpenMoji 17.0.0

Emojis designed by [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org/), HfG Schwäbisch Gmünd and contributors. Graphics licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). See LICENSE.txt.

Original metadata and selected PNGs are retained. Processed images are white-composited, aspect-preserving resized/padded to 512x512; derived images and contact sheets retain CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution. Authors and source URLs are in every manifest record. Captions use upstream annotations plus a constant style phrase, except reviewed E148 (broom and dustpan) replacing the culturally specific kehrwoche.
